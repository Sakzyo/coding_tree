### Important

1. **Owner-scope disposal releases shell ownership before the shell finishes cleanup.** `accept` forks the release scope directly under the Runner owner at `packages/opencode/src/effect/runner.ts:88`, but `startShell` forks its actual work with `Effect.forkChild` at `:172`, under the calling fiber rather than that owner scope. SessionRunState registers its owner finalizer calling `runner.cancel` before accepting work (`packages/opencode/src/session/run-state.ts:46`). LIFO disposal therefore closes the newer accepted scope and releases the claim first; only afterward does the earlier Runner finalizer interrupt the shell and wait for its actual cleanup. The focused probe of the real Runner, with that exact registration ordering and a Deferred-gated shell finalizer, printed `{"heldDuringShellCleanup":0,"disposalPending":true}`. The required result is ownership retained while disposal is pending. `SessionPrompt.shell` has an additional caller-scoped claim (`packages/opencode/src/session/prompt.ts:1385`) that can mask this on that entry path, but it does not satisfy the independent engine/SessionRunState lifetime seam required by the brief. Tie the shell fiber interruption/join to an outer lifetime which closes before its release, preserving existing shell caller-cancellation semantics; add a deterministic owner-disposal regression. Review the analogous parent-scope ordering for other accepted work while implementing the fix, rather than relying on status or incidental outer claims.
